# Muslim (مسلم) Constitution

These are the binding rules for everyone, human or AI agent, who contributes to the Muslim app.
The key words MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD and MAY are used as in RFC 2119. Where a rule here conflicts
with a spec, plan, task, tool default or personal preference, this document wins.

## Core Principles

### I. Quran Text Integrity (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

Any error in the Quran text is unacceptable, so the text is treated as immutable imported data.

- Quran text, ayah numbering, and Mushaf page and line layout MUST come only from the pinned
  import of a trusted source: the Quran.com API or data published by the King Fahd Complex for
  the Printing of the Holy Quran.
- Quran text MUST NOT be typed, pasted, edited, auto-corrected, re-encoded, normalised or
  generated by a person or an AI model anywhere in the repository. This covers source code,
  tests, fixtures, UI strings, documentation, commit messages and PR descriptions. Tests refer
  to ayahs by key (for example `2:255`) and by checksum, never by literal text.
- The import MUST be a reproducible script. Every imported artifact MUST be listed in a
  provenance manifest with its source URL, retrieval date and SHA-256 checksum.
- CI MUST verify on every PR that the bundled dataset matches the manifest checksums and the
  structural invariants: 114 surahs, 6236 ayahs, 604 pages, 30 juz, 60 hizb.
- The app MUST display the canonical text byte-for-byte as imported. Search MAY use a separate
  search index that comes from the source or is derived from a source field by a documented,
  tested, mechanical rule. A search index MUST NOT be shown as the ayah text.
- Quran text MUST be rendered only with the font that matches its encoding. If that font
  cannot be loaded, the app MUST show an error state instead of falling back to another font.
- After the initial import, any change to the bundled Quran dataset MUST be its own PR, MUST
  include a summary of what changed, and MUST be approved by the project owner.

Rationale: a typed or silently altered ayah is the one defect this product can never ship.

### II. Religious Content Safeguards (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

- The app and its content MUST NOT depict any prophet in any form: drawing, animation, actor,
  avatar, silhouette presented as the prophet, AI-generated likeness or impersonated voice.
  Videos use narration with maps and illustrations of places and events.
- Every story script, video, quiz and explanatory text MUST be reviewed by a qualified
  specialist before publication. Each catalog item carries a review status, and the app MUST
  surface only items marked as approved.
- Stories MUST rely on the Quran and the authentic Sunnah, without Isra'iliyyat.
- Tafsir and other scholarly text MUST come from named published works, shown unaltered and
  attributed to their author. Contributors and AI agents MUST NOT write, paraphrase or
  summarise tafsir, hadith, rulings or story content and present it as app content.
  Placeholder religious content MUST NOT be invented for demos, tests or screenshots.
- The Mushaf screen MUST NOT contain ads, promotions or anything that interrupts reading or
  recitation.

Rationale: users trust the app with their religion; unreviewed content breaks that trust.

### III. Licensed, Attributed Sources

- Every third-party asset (Quran text, tafsir, audio, fonts, video, icons) MUST have an entry
  in the sources registry with its origin, license or terms of use, required attribution and a
  verification status.
- The app MUST include a Sources page generated from that registry.
- An asset whose terms are not yet verified MAY be used in development builds, but a public
  store release MUST NOT ship until every registry entry is marked verified by the project
  owner. This covers the audio recitation terms in particular.

Rationale: the content belongs to others, and it is used with permission and credit.

### IV. Offline-First and Private by Default

- Quran text and the default tafsir MUST be fully usable with no network and no account.
- Reading position, bookmarks, memorisation marks and settings MUST be stored on the device
  only. The MVP has no accounts and no backend.
- The app MUST NOT include analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. Adding one requires a
  constitution amendment.
- Network access is limited to streaming audio, streaming video and refreshing the content
  catalog. A network failure MUST degrade to a clear, recoverable state and MUST NOT block
  reading.

### V. A Calm, Accessible Reading Experience

- The app is Arabic-first and right-to-left. Every screen MUST work in RTL.
- The Mushaf screen is for reading: controls stay out of the way until asked for, and page
  turning and scrolling MUST hold 60 fps on a mid-range Android phone.
- Every theme, including black-on-white and white-on-black, MUST keep body text at a contrast
  ratio of at least 7:1 and MUST be applied to the whole app.
- Interactive elements MUST have a touch target of at least 48 by 48 dp and a screen reader
  label. Text outside the fixed Mushaf page layout MUST respect the user's font size setting.
- Visual design follows `DESIGN.md` and the Impeccable skill. Colours, type and spacing MUST
  come from design tokens, not ad hoc values in widgets.

### VI. Spec-Driven, Tested, Reviewed Delivery

- Every change MUST trace to a task in `specs/<feature>/tasks.md`. Work that has no task gets
  a task first.
- Each task, or a small group of tightly coupled tasks, ships as its own pull request with a
  description of what changed and how it was verified.
- A PR MUST pass formatting, static analysis with zero warnings, and the full test suite
  before it is merged. Tests MUST NOT be skipped, disabled or weakened to get a green build.
- Data import, parsing, domain logic and persistence MUST have unit tests. Each user story's
  acceptance scenarios MUST be covered by widget or integration tests.
- Every PR MUST be reviewed by someone other than its author, human or AI, against the spec
  and this constitution. Review findings are fixed before merge, not deferred silently.

### VII. Simplicity

- Build the MVP defined in the spec and nothing beyond it. Post-MVP ideas stay out of the code
  until they are specified.
- Prefer the platform and the standard library over a new dependency. Each new dependency
  MUST be justified in the PR that adds it.
- No abstraction without two real uses. No backend, account system or sync layer until a spec
  requires one.

## Technology and Platform Constraints

- One Flutter codebase on the stable channel, targeting Android and iOS. Android is the first
  release target.
- The technology stack, package choices and project structure are recorded in the active
  feature's `plan.md`. Changing them requires updating the plan in the same PR.
- Secrets, keys and signing material MUST NOT be committed. The MVP needs none.
- Remote content the app loads at runtime (audio, video, catalog) MUST be fetched over HTTPS
  from hosts listed in the sources registry.
- User-facing strings MUST live in localisation files, with Arabic as the primary locale and
  English as the second. Quran text is data, not a localised string.

## Development Workflow and Quality Gates

1. **Specify, plan, tasks.** Features follow the Spec Kit flow: constitution, spec, plan,
   tasks, implement. The plan's Constitution Check MUST pass before implementation starts.
2. **Branch per task.** Work happens on a short-lived branch named for the task and is merged
   by squash through a PR. Nobody pushes directly to `main` after the bootstrap commit.
3. **Definition of done for a PR:**
   - the task's acceptance criteria are met and its box in `tasks.md` is ticked;
   - `dart format` reports no changes, `flutter analyze` reports no issues, `flutter test`
     passes;
   - the dataset integrity check passes;
   - UI changes include a rendered capture (golden image or screenshot) checked against
     `DESIGN.md`;
   - an independent review has no open findings.
4. **Review checklist.** Every review confirms Principles I and II explicitly: no Quran text
   literals were introduced, no religious content was authored, and no prophet is depicted.
5. **Release gates.** A public release additionally requires: all sources verified
   (Principle III), all shipped catalog items approved (Principle II), and the project owner's
   sign-off.

## Governance

- This constitution supersedes all other project practices. Specs, plans and tasks MUST comply
  with it, and a reviewer who finds a violation MUST block the PR.
- The project owner (Mazen) approves amendments. An amendment is a PR that changes this file,
  explains the reason, and updates any spec, plan or template it affects.
- Versioning follows semantic versioning: MAJOR for removing or redefining a principle, MINOR
  for adding a principle or materially expanding guidance, PATCH for clarifications.
- Principles I and II are non-negotiable: they cannot be waived for a deadline, a demo or a
  test, and any exception is itself an amendment.
- A justified deviation from Principles III to VII MUST be recorded in the plan's Complexity
  Tracking table in the PR that introduces it.
